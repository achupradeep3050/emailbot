Hermes Emailbot

Hermes Emailbot is a private email assistant run by Achu Pradeep for his own mailboxes (his personal Gmail and the Gmail of Breakout International Academy, Kochi). It reads new mail, picks out the messages that need attention, and sends a short summary of those to its owner on Telegram. The owner can then read, archive, mark as read, or approve a reply from Telegram.

It is not a public service: it does not accept sign-ups, and only its owner's accounts are connected.
